Our story

From a scattered inbox to one calm workspace

In 2019, three engineers in Tamil Nadu were spending more time hunting for messages, files and decisions than actually building. Chat lived in one app, documents in another and tasks in a third.

So they built Stackly: one place where conversations, docs, meetings and project boards stay connected, no matter where teammates live or which time zone they work in.

Our journey

Seven years of building in the open

A few milestones that shaped Stackly into what it is today.

2019

Three friends, one frustration

Founded in Tamil Nadu by three engineers tired of juggling chat, docs and task tools across time zones.

2020

The first 100 teams

Our private beta grew by word of mouth as distributed work became the new normal.

2021

Video and live docs

Launched HD meetings with auto-transcripts and real-time shared documents.

2023

SOC 2 Type II

Completed our independent security audit and introduced SSO, roles and audit logs.

2025

Ten thousand teams

Crossed ten thousand teams across more than forty countries, with support around the clock.

2026

Insights arrives

Shipped Insights so leaders can see workload balance, response times and delivery trends.
