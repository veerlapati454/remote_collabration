FAQ

Before you write to us

A few quick answers that might save you time.

How fast will I get a reply?

Most messages are answered within a few hours on working days, and always within one working day.

Do you offer support outside office hours?

Yes. Our human support team is available around the clock for paid plans, including weekends.

Can I get a personalised demo?

Of course. Choose "Book a demo" in the form and we will schedule a 20-minute walkthrough for your team.

Where can I find billing help?

Choose "Billing question" in the form, or manage invoices and plans from your billing settings.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. We only use your details to answer your enquiry and never share them with third parties.
